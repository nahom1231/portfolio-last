Questions

Before we talk.

Are you a full-stack developer?

No, I'm a frontend developer. I build the interface and connect it to your REST API or CMS. For backend work I'm happy to collaborate with your team or a partner.

What do you work with?

React and Next.js with modern JavaScript, HTML and CSS, plus GSAP for scroll and motion work. I deploy to platforms like Cloudflare Pages.

Do you work with clients outside Ethiopia?

Yes. I'm in Addis Ababa (UTC+3) and work remotely with local small businesses and international teams, with clear written updates along the way.
